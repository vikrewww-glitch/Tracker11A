Track a moving object frame by frame. Position, velocity and acceleration are computed on your device — your video never leaves it.

For best results

  • Keep the phone still — use a tripod or rest it on something.
  • Point the camera straight at the plane of motion.
  • Include an object of known length, like a meter stick.
  • Use good lighting and a plain, contrasting background.
